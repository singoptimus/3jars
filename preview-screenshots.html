My pick for Facebook ad traffic

Option 2 (2×2 static grid with captions). Reasons: (1) ALL four game types are visible instantly — parents see breadth in one glance. (2) Captions translate "what you see" into "why it matters to my kid". (3) Static = fast, no JS dependencies, works on every device. (4) Facebook's algorithm rewards pages where visitors scroll + stay; a grid gets both.

Option 1 (carousel) is a strong second — feels App Store-native on mobile and looks sharp. Use it if mobile is >90% of your traffic.

Option 3 (tilted stack) looks premium in Dribbble screenshots but can feel messy on small phones. Good for a "hero" section below the fold.

Option 4 (single rotating phone) is only worth it if you have an actual video or animated walkthrough — for static screenshots, it's less informative per second.

Where to put the section in the page flow: Right after "How it works" and before "The 3 Jars" reward detail. The current order reads: Hero → How it works → The 3 Jars → Why Parents Love → FAQ → Games list. Inserting Screenshots between How it works and The 3 Jars turns the flow into: Hero → How (loop) → What (screens) → Why (rewards) → Social proof → FAQ. That "what does the product actually look like?" question gets answered right when the parent is primed to ask it.
