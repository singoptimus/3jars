Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

3 Jars Academy (“3 Jars,” “we,” “our”) is a free web app where kids practice math, languages, and puzzles and earn points they split across three jars — Family, Investing, and Giving. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Questions? Email singolda@gmail.com.

Who runs this

3 Jars Academy is a personal project by Adam Singolda. It is not a company. If you need to reach a real person, the email above is the fastest way.

What we collect

When you create an account:

  • Email address (used as your account identifier)
  • Display name (either what you type, or auto-derived from your email, or provided by Google/Facebook/X if you sign in with those)
  • Password hash (we never store the password itself, only a one-way hash)
  • Preferred second language (e.g., Hebrew, Spanish — used inside the games)
  • Sign-in provider (if you used Google, Facebook, or X: the provider name and the provider’s user ID, so we can recognize you next time)

As kids play:

  • “Players” you create inside your account (each kid has a name; you can add or remove them anytime)
  • Game scores, completed levels, and play history per player
  • Which games each player has used and how often

Automatically:

  • Basic browser info needed to make the site work (browser type, screen size, timestamps of visits). We do not use analytics services like Google Analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Errors and crashes, if they occur, logged to the browser console only.

What we do NOT collect

  • Payment information — the service is free, there is nothing to charge for.
  • Physical addresses, phone numbers, birthdays, gender, or any other personal fields we don’t ask you for.
  • Location beyond what your browser exposes (which we don’t look at).
  • Microphone, camera, or file access.

Why we collect it

Your email + password let you sign back in. Scores and play history let the games remember progress and award points. The second-language preference picks which language the games use. That’s it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We do not run ads. We share only with the services we rely on to make 3 Jars work:

  • Google Firebase — hosts the real-time database where your account and scores live, and handles authentication (including Google / Facebook / X sign-in).
  • Twilio SendGrid — sends the daily email digest to people who want one.
  • EmailJS — fires a notification to the site owner when a new user signs up.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the static HTML/JS/CSS files.

Each of these services has its own privacy terms. By using 3 Jars you accept that your data will flow through them as needed for the service to function.

Children’s privacy

3 Jars is designed for parents to set up an account, then let their kids play under that account as “players.” The account holder (you, the adult) is in control of the data. Kids should not create accounts directly without a parent.

If we become aware that someone under 13 has created an account for themselves, we will delete it. If you’re a parent or guardian and believe your child set up an account, email singolda@gmail.com and we’ll remove it.

Cookies and local storage

We store a few things in your browser’s localStorage so you don’t have to sign in every visit: your current account email, a backup of the account key, and a local copy of your account data that’s also synced to Firebase. You can clear these by using your browser’s site-data controls.

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics cookies.

Your rights

You can:

  • See what we have about you — email singolda@gmail.com from the address on your account and we’ll send you everything we store, within 30 days.
  • Correct anything — most fields are editable in Settings after you log in. For anything you can’t self-serve, email us.
  • Delete your account and all associated data — see our data-deletion page for the exact steps. Deletion is permanent and takes up to 30 days to propagate across all our backends.

If you’re in a region with stronger rights (EU/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA, etc.), those rights apply automatically and we’ll honor them.

Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, not plaintext. Data in transit uses HTTPS. The Firebase database uses access rules that prevent users from reading or writing to other users’ accounts. No system is bulletproof, but we take reasonable care.

International transfers

Firebase, SendGrid, and EmailJS are U.S.-based services. If you’re using 3 Jars from outside the U.S., your data is transferred to and processed in the U.S. By using the service, you consent to that transfer.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we’ll update the “Last updated” date at the top and, where we can, email existing users. Minor wording tweaks that don’t change the meaning won’t trigger a notice.
