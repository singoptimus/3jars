Delete Your Data

Last updated: April 18, 2026

You can delete your 3 Jars Academy account and everything associated with it at any time. Here’s how.

Request deletion by email

Send an email to singolda@gmail.com from the email address on your account. Include one sentence: “Please delete my 3 Jars account.”

Email us to delete →

Heads up: deletion is permanent. Scores, players, progress, and settings won’t come back. If you just want to stop the daily email digest, reply to any digest email with “unsubscribe” instead.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record (email, display name, password hash, language preference)
  • All players you created inside the account
  • All scores, play history, and game progress
  • Any social-login associations (Google, Facebook, X)
  • Your entry on any email digest list

How long it takes

We act on the request within 30 days, typically much faster. You’ll get an email reply confirming when the deletion is done.

Deleting from the social-login side

If you signed in with Facebook or X and want to also revoke the 3 Jars app’s access on their side:

  • Facebook: Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → find “3 Jars Academy” → Remove.
  • X (Twitter): Settings and privacy → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps → “3 Jars Academy” → Revoke access.
  • Google: myaccount.google.com → Security → Your connections to third-party apps → “3 Jars Academy” → Remove.

Revoking on those platforms stops them from sharing data with 3 Jars in the future, but to fully delete data already stored on 3 Jars’ side you still need to email us as described above.

Questions

Email singolda@gmail.com — we’ll reply within a few days.
